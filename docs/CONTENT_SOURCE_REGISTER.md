# Content Source Register — Bakaiya Krishi Saathi

All farmer-facing crop, livestock, media, video, and helpline records must map to an authoritative Nepali agricultural/veterinary source or Creative Commons licensed repository.

## 1. Crop & Agronomy Sources
| Crop Category | Verified Primary Sources | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Cereals (Paddy, Maize) | Nepal Agricultural Research Council (NARC), National Maize Research Program Rampur, Krishi Diary, FAO EcoCrop | Varieties, ecological zones, soil pH, sowing/harvest windows |
| Vegetables (Cauliflower, Tomato) | National Vegetable Crop Development Center, NARC Horticulture Division, AITC Nepal | Nursery, transplanting, tunnel cultivation, IPM |
| Spices, Pulses, Oilseeds, Fruits (Ginger, Lentil, Mustard, Banana) | National Ginger Research Program Kapurkot, Grain Legumes Research Program Khajura, Oilseed Research Program | Climate ranges, disease risk notes, post-harvest handling |

## 2. Livestock & Fishery Sources
| Species | Verified Sources | Safety Policy |
|---|---|---|
| Cattle, Buffalo, Goat, Chicken, Pig, Fish | Krishi Gyan Kendra Makwanpur, Veterinary Hospital & Livestock Service Expert Center Makwanpur, NARC Livestock & Pasture Research | Quantitative drug dosages and individualized feed formulations are intentionally omitted; farmers are directed to local veterinary technicians (`पशु सेवा प्राविधिक`). |

## 3. Verified Educational Videos & Images
- **Videos**: Verified public Nepali channels (`AITC Nepal`, `Krishi Television HD`, `NTV NEWS NEPAL`) validated via `youtube.com/oembed`. YouTube videos open in the YouTube app/web (`downloadPermission = false`); only explicit authorized MP4 files support offline local caching.
- **Images**: Verified Creative Commons Wikimedia Commons imagery with explicit license attribution in SQL Migration 004 and `BakaiyaLivestockSeedData.kt`.
